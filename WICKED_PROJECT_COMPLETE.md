## 🎯 Key Findings That Challenge Assumptions

### 1. Duration Doesn't Matter
**Myth:** "Shorter songs perform better in the streaming era"  
**Reality:** r=0.05, p=0.80 (zero correlation)  
**Quote:** "Stop cutting good art to fit arbitrary time limits that the data doesn't support"

### 2. "For Good" Beats "Defying Gravity"
**Expectation:** Big showstopper wins  
**Reality:** Quiet duet (58) > dramatic finale (56)  
**Insight:** "Listeners prefer crying to their coffee over belting high notes"

### 3. Three Song Archetypes
**Discovery:** Epic Showstoppers, Narrative Builders, International Variants  
**Insight:** "Filler tracks" are structural necessities, not failures

### 4. Consistent Quality
**Finding:** Mean (46.9) ≈ Median (49.0)  
**Insight:** Not a "one-hit wonder" album
