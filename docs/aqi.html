AIR QUALITY QUICK REPORT

Air Quality Intelligence

Real-time environmental monitoring with AI-powered health recommendations

Overview

An intelligent agent system combining the Model Context Protocol (MCP) with real-time air quality data to provide personalized health recommendations for outdoor activities. Built with Python, async architecture, and the API Ninjas Air Quality API.

Global Coverage

6 Pollutants Tracked

15 min Update Frequency

Live Real-Time Data

Key Features

Real-Time Monitoring

Live AQI data for cities worldwide with detailed pollutant breakdown (PM2.5, PM10, O3, NO2, SO2, CO)

Smart Recommendations

Activity-specific advice for jogging, cycling, walking, and outdoor sports tailored to current conditions

Multi-City Compare

Side-by-side analysis of air quality across multiple cities for travel planning

Health Protection

Special guidance for sensitive groups including children, elderly, and those with respiratory conditions

Health Categories & Color Coding

The system uses the EPA Air Quality Index (AQI) standard to categorize air quality levels:

Good (0-50)

Air quality is excellent. Safe for all outdoor activities. No health concerns for any population group.

Moderate (51-100)

Air quality is acceptable. Unusually sensitive individuals should consider limiting prolonged outdoor exertion.

Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups (101-150)

Sensitive groups (children, elderly, people with respiratory conditions) should reduce prolonged outdoor activities.

Unhealthy (151-200)

Everyone should avoid prolonged outdoor activities. Sensitive groups should stay indoors.

Very Unhealthy (201-300)

Health alert! Everyone should avoid all outdoor activities. Emergency precautions recommended.

Hazardous (301+)

Health emergency! Everyone should remain indoors. Seek immediate medical attention if experiencing symptoms.

Core Components

1. AQI MCP Server (aqi_mcp_server.py) - Asynchronous API integration with aiohttp - Geocoding for city → GPS coordinate conversion - Real-time air quality data retrieval - Health category classification (Good, Moderate, Unhealthy, etc.) - Activity-specific recommendation engine

2. Interactive Demo (aqi_demo.py) - Two modes: Interactive and Quick Report - Multi-city comparison with rankings - Color-coded terminal output - Comprehensive error handling

3. Data Model (AQIData class)

Gen AI Conversational Agent

Building on the core MCP architecture, this project features AI-powered natural language understanding using OpenAI GPT. The Gen AI agent transforms raw air quality data into personalized, contextual health advice through natural conversations.

What Makes It Gen AI

Natural Language Understanding

Ask questions in plain English with no commands to memorize. The AI understands context and intent.

Personalized Recommendations

Tailored advice based on your health conditions, activities, and concerns (asthma, allergies, age, etc.)

Contextual Intelligence

Combines real-time data with medical knowledge, considers multiple factors for nuanced advice

Conversation Memory

Maintains context across multi-turn conversations for follow-up questions and clarifications

Live Gen AI Conversations

Example 1: Simple Air Quality Check

User: What’s the air quality like in Boston today?

AI Assistant: Great news. The air quality in Boston is currently excellent with an AQI of 47, which falls in the Good category.

Here’s what this means for you: Air is safe for everyone. All outdoor activities are perfectly fine. PM2.5: 0.7 µg/m³ (very low particulate matter) Ozone: 56.3 µg/m³ (within safe limits)

This is ideal weather for outdoor activities. Whether you want to jog, cycle, or spend time in the park, the air quality will not be a concern today. Enjoy the fresh air.

Key Difference: The Gen AI agent provides empathetic, conversational responses that explain why and what to do, not just data dumps.

Example 2: Health-Specific Personalized Advice

User: I have asthma. Can I go jogging in Dhaka today?

AI Assistant: I would advise caution for jogging in Dhaka today. The current AQI is 116, which is in the Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups category.

Since you have asthma, here is what I recommend:

Today’s Conditions: AQI: 116 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups) PM2.5: 48.9 µg/m³ (elevated particulate matter) PM10: 58.6 µg/m³ (elevated)

Jogging Recommendations: 1. Best option: Exercise indoors today (gym, treadmill, indoor track) 2. If you must jog outside: Go early morning or late evening when AQI is typically better Keep it short (15-20 minutes max) Stay at a comfortable pace, avoid intense exertion Carry your inhaler Monitor how you feel, stop if you experience any symptoms

  1. Watch for symptoms: Coughing, wheezing, shortness of breath, or chest tightness mean you should stop immediately

Better days ahead: Check the AQI forecast, it might improve in the next day or two. Your health comes first.

Intelligence at Work: The AI understands the user has a pre-existing condition and provides risk assessment specific to asthma, tiered recommendations (best to acceptable), symptom monitoring guidance, and empathetic, safety-first messaging.
